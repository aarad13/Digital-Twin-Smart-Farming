# Farmer-friendly interface guide

The application has evolved from a farmer self-service screen into an **assisted village agriculture service**. Farmers are the beneficiaries; trained field workers and laboratory staff are the primary operators.

For the current workflow and updated screenshots/steps, see [ASSISTED_VILLAGE_SERVICE_GUIDE.md](ASSISTED_VILLAGE_SERVICE_GUIDE.md).

## Main areas

- **Village desk:** see service counts and choose a workspace.
- **Field team:** record a farmer's details after consent, schedule a visit, optionally capture farm GPS, link an existing virtual farm and create a traceable soil sample code/QR.
- **Soil lab:** record only measured results, attach a signed PDF/photo and explicitly mark the results verified after laboratory checking.
- **Farmer card:** show, print or read a simple summary. Only verified soil readings are presented as verified. The card directs the farmer to the agriculture officer/KVK for crop-specific interpretation.

## Optional Farm Intelligence tools

Regional Intelligence, Virtual Farm Builder and experimental Reports & Research remain available from the Village desk for staff or technical users. Farmers do not need to operate these screens.

## Language and accessibility

The main navigation and older introductory content support English and Hindi. Browser speech output is used for the farmer card when an appropriate language voice is available. Detailed scientific panels are still mostly English. Full regional-language translation and voice commands remain future work.

## Important limitations

This prototype does not have secure staff logins, organisation-level data separation, offline synchronisation, automatic soil-report OCR or live connections to government, KVK, FPO, fertiliser-company or laboratory systems. Partner integrations require formal approval and credentials. Use synthetic data until production authentication, privacy, security and audit controls are completed.
