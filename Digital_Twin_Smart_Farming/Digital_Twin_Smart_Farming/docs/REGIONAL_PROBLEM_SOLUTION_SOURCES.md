## Live screening source

The application screens Open-Meteo's seven-day forecast against a documented IMD rainfall terminology threshold: 64.5 mm or more in 24 hours is the start of the heavy-rainfall category. This is only a screening comparison; it is not an IMD warning. The IMD heat-wave criteria additionally require station/region-specific maximum temperature and departure-from-normal conditions, so the application only prompts users to check the official IMD bulletin rather than declaring a heat wave.

- IMD weather definitions: https://mausam.imd.gov.in/Forecast/marquee_data/Press%20Release%2030-04-2025.pdf
- ICAR-CRIDA crop contingency plans: https://www.icar-crida.res.in/Crop_Contingency_Plan.html

## Safe-use rules

1. A regional problem is not a diagnosis of an individual farm.
2. Current weather and SoilGrids signals are screening prompts; confirm through field inspection and local authorities.
3. Soil amendment, fertilizer, pesticide, variety and irrigation decisions must be based on field-specific records and locally approved recommendations.
4. SoilGrids mapped pH and texture do not replace field laboratory testing. The current regional layer does not infer available N, P, K or micronutrient deficiencies from map estimates.
5. For unprofiled states, broad zone-level context is labelled as such. The district contingency plan should be used for local recommendations.
